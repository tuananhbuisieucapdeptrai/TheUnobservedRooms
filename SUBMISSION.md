# Submission Copy

## Title

The Unobserved Rooms

## Short description

Observe a shifting quantum facility, braid two linked control histories into a valid route, and keep the faceless Surveyor in sight before it completes its inspection.

## Itch.io description

**The Unobserved Rooms** is a 10–15 minute first-person quantum-horror puzzle. Every threshold is uncertain until observed. Measuring a passage fixes it temporarily but consumes coherence, forcing you to decide which paths deserve to become real.

Explore a 14-room labyrinth, recover stabilizer crystals, and manipulate two entangled controls whose states change together. Meanwhile, the Surveyor moves through the facility. Looking directly at it slows its advance—but watching the threat makes navigation harder.

Created for the 2026 theme **quantum BRAIDing**, the solution is not a single switch: the player must physically travel between A and B and interleave operations on the correlated pair. Both stations must be calibrated and the final linked state must match the target, turning the player's route and input history into a braid.

The project is designed around three Moth Quantum engines: `labyrinth-v1` for topology, `graph-v1` for the linked A/B relationship, and `comet-qrng-v1` for bounded event variation. The jam build includes a deterministic cached demonstration payload and a transparent Quantum Run Report after every result.

## Controls

- WASD: move
- Mouse: look
- Shift: sprint
- Hold E: interact or observe
- F: flashlight
- Esc: release/capture cursor
- R: retry after victory or defeat

## Accessibility and content

- No gore or jumpscare images.
- Contains pursuit, darkness, flickering illumination, and low-frequency horror audio.
- Critical paths use illuminated floor guidance and high-contrast interaction prompts.
- Keyboard and mouse required in the current jam build.
